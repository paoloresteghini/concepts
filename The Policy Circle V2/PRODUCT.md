# The Policy Circle

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototypes. A chosen direction will later become a bespoke WordPress theme. Keep semantic sections, source content, and program links easy to map into editable WordPress blocks and fields.

## Users
Women exploring public policy, community conversations, and civic leadership. Secondary paths serve existing members, donors, and military community participants.

## Product Purpose
The Policy Circle is a national, nonpartisan 501(c)(3) that informs, equips, and connects women to be more impactful citizens and influential local leaders.

## Positioning
Accessible policy resources, member-led roundtable conversations, and practical leadership programs turn civic interest into local action. The official organization describes its values in terms of personal and economic freedom. Nonpartisan does not mean that it has no stated principles.

## Operating Context
Visitors discover policy briefs, Policy Circles, CLER, military initiatives, member stories, events, and ways to support or connect with the organization.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Three materially different working homepage versions, each delegated to an implementation agent and reviewed by the lead through two improvement rounds. This is local design exploration, with no staging or production edits. No invented metrics, testimonials, events, partnerships, or organizational claims. Existing event area currently says new events will be announced soon.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the real logo, recognizable navy/rust/sand palette, authentic organization imagery, and factual homepage content. User wants exceptionally beautiful, richly animated, overlapping, interactive compositions with strong distinctions among versions. Never use em dashes.

## Evidence on Hand
research/staging-home.html, research/home-copy.txt, research/images.json, research/links.json. Source is https://policycirclstg.wpenginepowered.com/?review=nov4-final, reviewed November 4, 2026. Additional official research: https://www.thepolicycircle.org/why/ and https://www.thepolicycircle.org/about/.

## Product Principles
- Let real women and their civic action carry the story.
- Make a clear next action available without requiring animation or hover.
- Preserve content truth while inventing composition and interaction.
- Support mobile, keyboard navigation, and reduced motion.
