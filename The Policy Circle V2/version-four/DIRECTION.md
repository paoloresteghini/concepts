# The Open Forum

New direction commissioned after the user rejected The Civic Journal and Common Ground. This version does not revise either rejected visual world.

Bright white, precise connected photographic windows, deep navy, source ice-blue #b9cddb and its concept extension #eaf1f5. Moderate Montserrat display and Brandon Grotesque reading type. The source Seasons italic appears sparingly in the mission and newsletter. No cream, Bodoni, oversized serif, rounded atlas, or full-screen mission banner.

The top is an architectural gallery of three simultaneous current editorial priorities: a large Data Centers landscape, a Policy Circles conversation window with an ice-blue reading plane, and a CLER portrait with its caption entering from the left. These are three independently editable curated slots, separate from the Inform/Equip/Connect method below.

Visible content is the default. The image windows open as one restrained aperture sequence. Native scroll applies small opposed offsets within photo frames while caption planes remain stable. Hover/focus over method content changes its supporting image without hiding text. A true policy preview supports pointer, touch, arrow keys, Home/End, selected state, and destination changes. Reduced motion stops animated effects and scroll offsets.
