# The Civic Journal

An independent journal of civic life: warm paper, oversized expressive serif typography, fine navy rules, and rust invitations. The opening is an interleaved editorial spread rather than a conventional split hero. A photograph of a real Policy Circle conversation crosses the headline, and the phrase resolves beneath it.

The key interaction expands Inform, Equip, and Connect into a single editorial reading surface. Each choice changes the corresponding image and text. Photography opens with a clip reveal, then stays visible; the rest of the page varies among a navy policy index, a program image crossing the color boundary, a member feature, and an invitation.

The reusable body font is Brandon Grotesque, serif voice Bodoni Moda, and selective accent The Seasons italic, supplied by the shared assets/fonts.css. Supports reduced motion, keyboard controls, focus states, responsive navigation, real destinations, and source-based copy. All WordPress-sized content regions remain semantic sections with independently editable text and imagery.
