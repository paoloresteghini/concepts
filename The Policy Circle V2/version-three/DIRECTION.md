# Common Ground

A civic atlas with navy humanist typography, warm sand, and rust planes. A real conversation photograph meets the headline and a connected pathway rather than a conventional hero card. The pathway has three fully authored tab panels. The policy explorer has four real briefs with distinct previews and destinations. Sections meet through offset color planes, a leadership image crossing the navy/rust seam, and a member story crossing a shared typographic plane.

The page uses the existing organization logo, source photographs, and homepage copy. Only interface labels and editorial casing are new. It does not introduce metrics, testimonials, scheduled events, or fabricated forms. Program and newsletter links go to the provided organization source destinations. Source staging links are intentional in this local prototype.

## Motion and controls

- Desktop hero photography moves gently within the rust plane under native scroll.
- Changing pathway or brief redraws the image through a directional clip reveal.
- Tabs use roving focus, arrow keys, Home, End, selected state, and matching labelled panels.
- Navigation opens as a mobile menu with Escape and link selection handling.
- Reduced-motion users receive immediate complete state changes and no parallax.

## WordPress handoff

Independent semantic regions map to editable blocks: mission hero, pathway, policy explorer, CLER feature, member story, events empty state, newsletter invitation, and footer. Policy briefs and stories are natural dynamic queries; pathway content can use a repeatable group. No bundled framework or runtime dependency is required.
